# Spanish language pack for FFXIV (unofficial)

[Español](README.md) · **English**

Spanish translation of the Final Fantasy XIV text, built for the Dalamud plugin
**Gubal Library**.

This translation does not modify the game's original files.

> [!WARNING]
> **This project is not a professional localisation and does not aim to replace an official one.**
>
> It is a fan translation, non-commercial, with no connection whatsoever to Square Enix or its
> subsidiaries. The original text is the property of Square Enix.
>
> It is made mostly with AI under human supervision, so **there will be mistakes**.

## Why

This is an effort by the Spanish-speaking community: people who love the series and want to see it,
and share it, in their own language. A language spoken by some five hundred million people across
Spain and a score of countries in the Americas, and yet not one of the four in which Final Fantasy
XIV has been published for over a decade.

A good part of the series *has* been published in Spanish over more than twenty years, and that is
where the terms any fan recognises come from, newcomer or veteran alike: moguri, kupó (with the
accent :P), molbol, cactilio. May they end up filling Eorzea.

None of the above is a polite formula: this aims at something far more modest than an official
localisation and, in full transparency, it is largely made with AI, human supervision and the
affection of a group of fans of the series.

And should Square Enix ever wish to have this translation, we would gladly hand it over. Nothing
would please us more than to see Eorzea in Spanish officially, and this project no longer needed.

## Translation criteria

**Supervised AI translation with human review.** Given the volume of text, the bulk is translated by
language models following detailed instructions on terminology and judgement; the result is reviewed
and corrected.

**The series comes first.** Where a term has an official translation in any Final Fantasy published
in Spanish, that form takes precedence over any alternative. Examples: *moogle* is «moguri», *Limit
Break* is «Límite», *the Mist* is «la Niebla» (Final Fantasy XII) and *morbol* is «molbol» (Final
Fantasy XII *The Zodiac Age* and Final Fantasy VII Remake). Where a title was never published in
Spanish, as with Final Fantasy Tactics, there is no precedent to fall back on: the term is decided
here and documented as our own decision.

**Three reference languages, each with a distinct job.** Every line is translated with the English,
French and Japanese versions in view, and they are not three equivalent variants of the same
content:

- **English.** It is the text the player sees on screen, and it determines what is said.
- **Japanese.** It is Final Fantasy XIV's original version, and it supplies the intent, each
  character's register, the honorifics and the speech tics that English tends to flatten.
- **French.** Sharing grammatical structure with Spanish, it settles gender, number and address.
  English does not distinguish *tú* from *usted*; French does, so each character's formality can be
  inferred from it.

Divergence between the three versions is data, not an anomaly. Across much of the ambient content
the English team rewrote the dialogue while French and Japanese follow the original: in those cases
the translation is made from English, but the divergence is a warning that the scene was
reinterpreted and the tone is worth a second look.

**The source text is not a fixed sentence.** Many lines carry elements the game fills in as it
displays them: the player's name, their gender, a quantity. Spanish demands more agreement than
English, so the translation is written to work across every possible combination and not only the
one in front of us.

**Every tribe and faction has a voice of its own.** Sylphs speak of themselves in the third person,
kobolds articulate through the mask, the Sahagin hiss and goblins compress their words into
compounds. Those registers are fixed once and maintained from material already translated, rather
than reinvented with every quest.

### How to read these figures

**They are spread across all seven expansions at once**, so no row in the table tells you how much
of *your* playthrough is in Spanish. **A Realm Reborn is being finished completely before moving on
to the next**, and it runs well ahead of what the total suggests: in ARR the main scenario, the side
quests of the three city-states and **every class and job quest** are already done, from the first
quest at the guild to the end of each job, each with its dialogue, its journal, its objectives and
its callouts.

A new character starting today will find the game in Spanish almost from beginning to end through
the end of A Realm Reborn. From Heavensward onwards, the main scenario and the voiced scenes are
half done and the rest arrives in batches.

**Whatever is shown as finished is verified**, not estimated: every delivered line is checked
against the original text before it is published.
